FAQ

Questions?

How do I install Krypton?

Install Fabric Loader for your Minecraft version, download the Krypton .jar and drop it into your .minecraft/mods folder. Launch the Fabric profile and you're good to go.

Is Krypton free?

Yes. Krypton is completely free to download and use.

How do I open the menu?

Press Right Shift in game to open the ClickGUI. You can change this keybind in the settings.

Does it work with other mods?

Krypton plays nicely with most popular Fabric mods like Sodium and Iris.

Where can I report bugs or suggest features?

Join the Discord server — we read every suggestion and bug report.
